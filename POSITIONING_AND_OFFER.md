## 🛑 The Core Industry Problem

Real estate is a speed-to-lead game. Research shows that responding to an inbound lead within **5 minutes** increases conversion by **391%**, yet:

* **30%–40% of leads go cold** because agents are driving, negotiating, or hosting site visits.
* **45% of property inquiries** occur outside 9 AM – 6 PM business hours.
* **Over 60% of leads** never receive a second follow-up attempt.
* Manual lead entry into CRMs wastes **10+ hours per week** per agent.

We deploy specialized autonomous AI agents that plug seamlessly on top of existing CRMs (n8n, LangGraph, OpenAI, Vapi, WhatsApp Business API):

### 1. 💬 AI Lead-Qualifier Chatbot (WhatsApp / Web Widget)
- Qualifies budget, target location/neighborhood, purchase timeline, and buy-vs-rent.
- Pushes qualified leads directly to Google Sheets / CRM webhook.

### 2. 📞 AI Property Inquiry Voice Agent
- Answers after-hours incoming calls with human-like voice response.
- Provides listing details, pricing, amenities, and books site visits straight into Google Calendar.

### 3. 🔄 AI CRM Auto-Follow-Up System
- Automated 5-stage WhatsApp & Email drip sequence for cold/dormant leads.
- Re-engages cold buyers when new matching inventory drops.

### 4. 🔍 AI Property-Matching Engine (RAG)
- Vector search (Pinecone/Chroma + OpenAI) allowing natural language search (e.g. *"Show me 3 BHK apartments under 1.5Cr near Tech Park with pool"*).

### 5. 📅 AI Site-Visit Scheduler & WhatsApp Reminder Agent
- Sends instant WhatsApp directions upon booking.
- Automates 24-hr and 2-hr visit reminders with quick "Confirm / Reschedule" buttons to eliminate no-shows.
